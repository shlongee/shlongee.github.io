This site pairs [Astro](/tags/astro/) static pages with [Three.js](/tags/threejs/) and [WebGL](/tags/webgl/) shaders.
Here is how the pieces fit together:

## 1. Persistent Canvas via Astro ClientRouter
Jumping from **Home** to **Blog** never resets the aurora.
Astro’s `ClientRouter` swaps page content while `transition:persist` keeps the WebGL `<canvas>` running.

## 2. Fixing the Polar atan() Seam in GLSL
Raw `atan(y, x)` jumps sharply where `−π` wraps to `+π`.
Feeding seamless circle coords `(cosθ, sinθ, depth)`
into 3D noise with a gentle helical wobble heals the seam:

## 3. Instanced Three.js Title Particles
To render **SHLONGEELAND** in particles,
we draw the title to a 2D canvas,
spawn quads with `InstancedBufferGeometry`,
and shade each dot with a **core + halo** glow:

```glsl
float core = exp(-d * d * 6.5);
float halo = exp(-d * 2.4) * 0.58;
```
